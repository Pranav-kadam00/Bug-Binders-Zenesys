# Aqura

**Aqura** is an intelligent ERP platform focused on simplifying and automating business purchasing processes.

### Initial Focus

* Purchase Request Management
* Intelligent Vendor Recommendation
* Vendor Comparison
* Approval Workflow
* Purchase Order Management
* Order & Delivery Tracking
* Vendor Performance Analytics

### Vision

Aqura aims to evolve into a complete ERP platform integrating **Procurement, Inventory, Finance, HR, Sales, Supply Chain, and AI-powered Business Intelligence**.

**Smart Purchasing. Connected Business.**
